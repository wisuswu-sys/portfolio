Biography

Timeless methods,
future tools

Based in Licerio, Dalaya, I am Mark Aj V. Daud, a passionate designer and developer who thrives at the intersection of classic craftsmanship and modern technology. My journey began with a fascination for structured design in Microsoft Office tools, evolving into a deep appreciation for programming with C++ and VS Code.

I believe that true mastery comes from understanding both the art and the science behind every project. Whether I'm building elegant documents in Word, analyzing data in Excel, crafting compelling narratives in PowerPoint, or writing efficient code in C++ and VS Code, I bring the same level of dedication and classic sensibility to every endeavor.

I am drawn to projects that embrace narrative depth, materiality, and graceful aging — digital experiences that feel like heirlooms, built on solid foundations of technical excellence.
